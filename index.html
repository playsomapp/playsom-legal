Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PlaySom handles information when users access or use the PlaySom mobile application and related services.

PlaySom is an adult-only social discovery and random one-to-one communication service designed to connect adult users for live interaction.

1. Information PlaySom May Process

Depending on the features used and the production configuration of the service, information processed may include:

  • Phone number and authentication information.
  • Basic account and profile information voluntarily provided by users.
  • Age or adult-eligibility confirmation.
  • Account identifiers and internal user identifiers.
  • Matchmaking, queue and session information.
  • User reports, report reasons and safety-related information.
  • Blocked-user relationships.
  • Subscription or premium-entitlement status where applicable.
  • Technical, diagnostic, security and service-operation information.
  • Device or advertising identifiers where advertising services are enabled and permitted.

PlaySom aims to limit collection to information reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve the service and to satisfy applicable legal requirements.

2. Phone Authentication

PlaySom may use Firebase Authentication or another configured authentication provider for account authentication and phone-number verification.

Authentication providers may process the phone number and technical information necessary to authenticate the user and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is required for PlaySom's live video and audio communication functionality.

PlaySom requests these permissions through the device operating system. Users may manage these permissions through their device settings.

PlaySom does not claim ownership of users' live camera or microphone content.

4. Live Video and Audio

PlaySom may use Agora or another configured real-time communications provider to establish live audio/video sessions between matched users. Technical information necessary to establish, secure and maintain a session may be processed by the communications provider.

PlaySom does not provide an in-app screen-recording feature in the current service design.

However, PlaySom cannot prevent another participant from using device-level screen capture, an external camera, or other recording technology. Users should never disclose passwords, OTP codes, banking information, financial credentials, home addresses, identity documents or other highly sensitive information during an interaction.

5. Text Communication

Where text-chat functionality is available during a PlaySom session, information necessary to transmit and operate that communication may be processed as part of providing the service.

Users must not use text communication to distribute illegal, exploitative, threatening, hateful, fraudulent or otherwise prohibited material.

6. Matchmaking and Session Information

PlaySom may process information necessary to place users into a matchmaking queue, establish a match, maintain the session, prevent blocked or restricted matches and operate safety functionality.

7. Reports, Blocking and Safety Information

When a user reports or blocks another user, PlaySom may process and retain information associated with that action where reasonably necessary to operate safety systems, prevent repeat interactions, investigate abuse, enforce PlaySom policies, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations.

8. Advertising

Where advertising is enabled, PlaySom may use Google AdMob and related Google advertising technologies.

Google and its advertising technologies may process advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostics and related information according to applicable settings, consent requirements, law and Google's own policies.

9. Subscriptions and Payments

Where paid subscriptions or premium features are offered through the Android application, transactions may be processed through Google Play Billing.

PlaySom does not need to receive a user's full payment-card number from Google Play in order to determine applicable subscription or entitlement status. Google may independently process payment and transaction information under Google's terms and privacy practices.

10. Third-Party Service Providers

PlaySom may rely on third-party service providers where necessary to operate the service. Depending on the production configuration, these may include:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and related application services.
  • Agora — real-time audio/video communications.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, where enabled.
  • Google Play — application distribution, subscriptions and billing.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers required to operate PlaySom's backend services.

Third-party providers process information according to their own terms, privacy policies and applicable law.

11. How Information May Be Used

  • Creating and authenticating accounts.
  • Operating user profiles.
  • Providing matchmaking.
  • Providing live communication functionality.
  • Maintaining subscriptions or premium entitlements.
  • Displaying advertising where enabled.
  • Operating reports, blocks and safety systems.
  • Preventing fraud, spam, exploitation and abuse.
  • Maintaining security and service reliability.
  • Responding to support, privacy and safety requests.
  • Enforcing PlaySom policies and Terms of Service.
  • Complying with applicable laws and lawful requests.

12. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared or processed by service providers only where reasonably necessary to provide, secure, support or legally operate PlaySom.

PlaySom does not sell users' personal information as a standalone product.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users, investigate serious abuse, protect PlaySom's rights or address threats to safety.

13. Data Security

PlaySom uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service. Nevertheless, no internet transmission, database, mobile application or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, including operating the service, maintaining safety and security, preventing fraud or abuse, resolving disputes and satisfying applicable legal obligations.

Certain safety, fraud-prevention or legally required records may need to be retained for a limited period after an account-deletion request where there is a legitimate or legal reason to do so.

15. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their PlaySom account and associated personal data. Instructions are provided in the Delete Account & Data section of this page.

16. Adult-Only Service

PlaySom is not directed to children and does not permit users under the age of 18.

If PlaySom becomes aware that an account belongs to a person who is not eligible to use the service, appropriate action may be taken, including restriction or deletion where appropriate.

17. Privacy Choices

Users may manage applicable camera, microphone, notification and other device permissions through Android settings.

Users may also contact PlaySom regarding privacy questions or deletion requests using the contact information below.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as PlaySom's functionality, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective or last-updated date on this page will be revised when appropriate.

Child Safety Standards

PlaySom has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Under-18 Users Are Prohibited

People under 18 years of age are not permitted to create an account or use PlaySom.

2. CSAE Is Strictly Prohibited

PlaySom prohibits any content, communication or behavior that sexually exploits or abuses a child, facilitates such exploitation, sexualizes minors, grooms minors, solicits minors for sexual purposes, promotes child exploitation, or otherwise endangers a child.

3. CSAM Is Strictly Prohibited

Child sexual abuse material is prohibited. PlaySom does not permit users to create, request, distribute, transmit, promote or facilitate CSAM.

Users who encounter suspected underage use, child exploitation, grooming, CSAM or another serious child-safety concern should immediately end the interaction, use PlaySom's available in-app reporting mechanism and contact PlaySom's Child Safety Point of Contact.

If a child appears to be in immediate danger, users should contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement authority.

5. Response to Child-Safety Reports

PlaySom will take child-safety reports seriously and may investigate, restrict access, preserve information where legally required, remove accounts or take other appropriate action based on the circumstances and applicable law.

6. CSAM and Legal Reporting

Where PlaySom becomes aware of apparent CSAM or child sexual exploitation, PlaySom will take appropriate action consistent with applicable law, including preservation, removal, account action and reporting to the appropriate legally designated authority where required.
